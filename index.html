The KTH–LiU decentralized AI and Computing Workshop brings together researchers from KTH Royal Institute of Technology and Linköping University to share recent advances, exchange ideas, and foster collaborations in decentralized AI and distributed computing, with a focus on efficiency, privacy, and resilience.

Research themes.

01

Decentralized AI

Distributed learning, collaborative intelligence, and decentralized optimization across heterogeneous networks.

02

Distributed Computing

Communication-efficient computation, scalable coordination, and resource-aware algorithms for distributed systems.

03

Efficiency, Privacy & Resilience

Efficient learning and computation, privacy-preserving methods, and robustness against adversarial attacks.
