01 / INTRODUCTION

Ideas at the edge of computing.

The KTH–LiU Workshop on Efficient, Secure & Robust Distributed Intelligence brings together researchers from KTH Royal Institute of Technology and Linköping University for two days of exchange and discussion.

The workshop provides a forum to share research perspectives, identify common challenges, and explore opportunities for collaboration across decentralized AI and computing.
